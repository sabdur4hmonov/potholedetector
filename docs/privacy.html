Summary

Pothole Reporter is an independent app that finds potholes on roads in Uzbekistan. It has no project-operated server, advertising network or account system. Reports, photos, recordings and drive tracks you create stay on your phone until you delete them.

It is not fully offline: selected pothole photos and complete video frames are sent directly to OpenAI for analysis using your own API key, and precise coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to look up the street name. The app never sends a report to any authority, company or government service by itself. It is not a government service.

Data the app handles

DataWhat happensPurpose
Pothole photos and video frames A pothole photo, or selected complete frames sampled while Drive Mode runs, are resized (never cropped) and sent to the OpenAI API with your API key. On a later revisit to a known pothole, the saved earlier photo and current views may be sent together for a before/after repair check. Images are not blurred first and may contain faces, number plates, people, vehicles or shopfronts. Optional local video recording is off by default; recorded video is never uploaded by the app. Decide whether a visible pothole is present and keep evidence on the device.
Precise location and drive motion data The app reads latitude and longitude and keeps a local drive track with accuracy, speed, heading and timestamps. While Drive Mode runs, location is used in the background (a persistent notification is always shown), even when this app is not visible. Coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to look up a street name. Viewing the in-app map requests OpenStreetMap tiles for the displayed area. Raw GPS tracks are deleted automatically after 30 days; you can delete any drive earlier, or turn track keeping off in Settings. Pothole reports keep their own coordinates until you delete them. Tag potholes with a position and street, ignore repeat sightings of the same pothole and show trip statistics.
OpenAI API key On Android the key is encrypted with a non-exportable Android Keystore key and used only by native code for the fixed OpenAI request. It is never built into the app, shown again or sent to any project-operated server. The browser version keeps a key in page memory only. Authenticate usage billed to your own OpenAI account.
Reports, settings, photos, footage and drive summaries Stored on the device, including your language, model and Drive preferences. Run the app, keep history and let you review or share evidence.
Shared evidence When you choose Share evidence, the app hands a photo and a plain-text note (time, coordinates, map link, GPS accuracy) to Android’s share sheet. It goes only to the destination you choose. Let you pass a pothole to someone yourself.

External recipients

  • OpenAI: your API key, selected images and prompts. Requests set store: false, which prevents Responses API application-state retention but does not override OpenAI’s security, abuse-monitoring or legal retention. See OpenAI’s privacy policy. Processing can occur outside Uzbekistan.
  • OpenStreetMap services: coordinates sent to Nominatim for reverse geocoding and map-area requests sent to the tile service. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and Nominatim policy.
  • Your sharing providers: data is transferred only when you choose a destination in Android’s share sheet. Their own policies apply.

Like other internet services, these recipients can receive an IP address and standard connection metadata. The project does not control their retention or legal obligations.

Permissions

  • Camera captures a manual pothole photo or Drive frames. While Drive runs, the camera remains in use in the background, even when this app is not visible and another app such as Maps or a call is on screen, under a persistent foreground-service notification.
  • Precise or approximate location tags reports and records a drive track, in the background under the same notification.
  • Internet access reaches the services above.

Without an AI key, Drive uses the phone’s motion sensor (accelerometer, which needs no permission) to feel road shocks. The sensor readings are processed on the phone and are not stored or sent; only a shock’s location, speed, strength and the camera frame taken just before it are saved as a local report for you to review.

Road warnings (“pothole in 500 metres”) are worked out on the phone from the reports already stored on it. Spoken warnings use the phone’s own text-to-speech engine; the app sends nothing for them.

Community features are optional and off until you save a profile. If this app version is connected to the project's community server, you may choose to send: a display name and your region, district and village (shown in rankings); per-trip totals for rankings (day, distance, time, safe-driving score and event counts — never the route); pothole and road-shock coordinates (never photos); and anonymous speeds for traffic, with the first and last 300 metres of each trip removed and no identifier attached. “Delete my online data” removes your profile, trips and pothole confirmations from the server. Route search sends the destination text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the start and end points to the community server.

Antiradar mode uses only precise location, under its own persistent notification, to warn about cameras from a list you import and about your saved potholes. It does not use the camera and sends nothing; the camera list is stored on the phone until you remove it or delete all app data.

Voice navigation follows a route you searched for, under the same location-only notification. Turn instructions are worked out on the phone and spoken by its own text-to-speech engine. If you leave the route, the app asks the community server for a new route from your current position to the same destination.

The app does not request Android’s separate background-location permission. Drive can be paused or stopped from the app or the notification. Mount the phone before moving and do not interact with it while driving.

Retention and deletion

  • Reports, photos, repair evidence, sampled frames, drive summaries and any recorded footage remain in app-private storage until you delete them.
  • Raw GPS drive tracks are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can delete a single drive at any time, or turn track keeping off in Settings.
  • Drive has a selectable 15, 30, 60 or 90-minute active-time battery limit (default 30).
  • Delete all app data clears local reports, photos, drive summaries, footage, the API key and settings.
  • Files already shared, exported or copied elsewhere, and data already processed by OpenAI or OpenStreetMap services, are outside the app’s control.

There is no project account to delete. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Security and your choices

Internet calls use HTTPS. You can deny camera or location permission, but the corresponding feature will not work. You decide whether to enable recording, keep sampled frames, share evidence or delete data. AI can miss damage or produce false positives: review every result yourself.

Children, changes, and contact

Pothole Reporter is a road-reporting tool and is not designed for children.

If this policy or the app’s data flows change, this page will be updated and its effective date will change.

For a question or request, open a GitHub issue. Do not put an API key, precise location, unredacted photo or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Pothole Reporter is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body, OpenAI or OpenStreetMap.
