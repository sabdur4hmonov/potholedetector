# ML-DETECT-001

The training and integration deliverables are on `ondevice-ml`. The APK can run the existing sensor and warning flows without model assets. Colab training, model parity on real images and physical-phone validation await the owner-generated model.

- The [Colab notebook](../ml/train_pothole_yolox.ipynb) downloads the official 13.3 GB RDD2022 archive, prints and records its first measured SHA-256, and enforces the pin on later downloads. It uses annotated images in six road subsets, with D40 as the only positive class and a deterministic country and class split. It excludes China_Drone and the unlabelled challenge split. Every training and evaluation image uses the complete field of view. The training loader bypasses mosaic, mixup and affine augmentation.
- The notebook trains Nano from scratch with resumable Drive checkpoints. Validation chooses the best F1 threshold with precision at least 0.80. The held-out test reports precision, recall, F1 and recall per country. The export checks PyTorch/ONNX output on 20 test frames and writes both required files. See [training instructions](../ml/README.md) and [licenses](../MODEL_LICENSE.md).
- Android loads only bundled assets within size limits. It verifies SHA-256, manifest and tensor shapes, then uses one CPU worker and no bitmap queue. Three complete frames use BGR 0–255 letterbox to 416 by 416. At least two Yes results produce a camera report. Average per-view latency appears in the debug HUD and the sensor CSV. Boxes stay inside native inference results.
- The sensor adapter retains at most 16 recent camera hits and 16 recent bumps. A camera observation and a later bump 150–6,000 ms apart within 40 m can corroborate one report. Same-drive duplicates within 15 m use the existing evidence ownership and private outbox. Camera-only and bump-only reports use possible warnings. Corroborated reports use confirmed warnings. Owner labels and fixed state remain authoritative in the web snapshot. GPS evidence approximates where the frame or bump was recorded.
- Correct and Wrong preserve full frames. Wrong removes warning eligibility. Labelled ZIP files omit GPS metadata by default and re-encode the full frame to strip embedded metadata. An owner negative can train directly. An owner positive needs human boxes to supervise object detection. Unboxed positives wait for annotation rather than becoming negatives.
- The native video share attaches a GPX and adds UTC start/end and Maps links when the finished drive has an available, opted-in track. GPX lives in the existing narrow share cache and follows track deletion, expiry checks, opt-out and Delete All. A recipient's copy is outside app storage.

The exact next task is to run Colab, retain the measured archive pin and metrics, and install the exported model and manifest in `android-app/android/app/src/main/assets/ml/`. Verify the real-runtime fixture results before building the model-bearing APK and checking field behavior. No model weights or datasets are included in Git.
